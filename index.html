About the Practitioner

Dr. He (Annie) Li
DAIM, L.Ac.

Dr. He (Annie) Li is a California Licensed Acupuncturist and Doctor of Acupuncture and Integrative Medicine. Her unique background combines over ten years practicing as a physician in a leading OB/GYN hospital in China with advanced training in Traditional Chinese Medicine.

She believes true health is not just about normal physical indicators, but about the holistic balance of emotions, lifestyle, and physical wellbeing. She takes the time to listen, understand, and create a plan built around you — never a one-size-fits-all approach.
