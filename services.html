Treatment Modalities

Dr. Li draws on a full spectrum of Traditional Chinese Medicine therapies, often combining multiple modalities within a single personalized treatment plan.

🪡

Acupuncture

Hair-fine sterile needles are placed at specific points along the body's meridians to regulate the flow of Qi, reduce pain, and support healing.

🌿

Chinese Herbal Formulas

Time-tested herbal combinations are custom-prescribed based on your TCM pattern diagnosis, supporting treatment between sessions.

🔵

Cupping

Suction cups placed on the skin increase local blood circulation, release muscle tension, and draw out stagnation.

🔥

Moxibustion

Dried mugwort herb is burned near acupuncture points to warm the meridians, strengthen Yang Qi, and tonify deficiency.

✋

Meridian Bodywork

Therapeutic massage and pressure applied along meridian pathways to release blockages, ease tension, and restore flow.

🥗

Medicinal Diet Guidance

Dietary and lifestyle recommendations informed by TCM principles — using food as a natural extension of your treatment plan.
